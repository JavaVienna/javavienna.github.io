Moritz Becker is software developer with more than 3 years of professional experience in the Java EE ecosystem. Besides his job at Linz based Curecomp GmbH and his work for various open source projects like Blaze-Persistence, he is doing his master's degree in Software Engineering & Internet Computing at the University of Technology Vienna. Previously, he was co-founder & developer at Vienna based Sweazer GmbH where he was concerned with co-developing a mobile multi-channel shopping app.
